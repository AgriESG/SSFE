AgriESG Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

In short: AgriESG has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. We don't collect your name, email, location or any other personal details. Your preferences and basket history stay on your phone.

Who we are

AgriESG ("we", "us") makes the AgriESG app, which helps you compare the cost, nutrition and environmental impact of your food basket and suggests better swaps. For any privacy question, email info@agriesg.co.uk.

What stays on your device

The app saves the following on your phone only:

  • Your preferences: diet type, allergies, foods you dislike, budget, sustainability priority, nutrition goal, household size and display detail level
  • Your basket history (previous baskets and their results)

We can't see this data. Deleting the app removes it.

What is sent to our server

To compare baskets and suggest swaps, the app sends the following to our server (agriesg-api.onrender.com):

  • The foods in your basket and any food searches you type
  • Your preferences (listed above), so suggestions respect your diet and allergies
  • Whether you accepted or dismissed a suggested swap

This is used to work out your results and then discarded. We don't store your basket, searches or preferences, and nothing sent is linked to you or your device.

Anonymous usage counts

To understand how the app is used, our server keeps simple totals such as "number of basket comparisons today" or "how often swap X was accepted". These are counts only. They contain no identifiers and can't be traced back to any person or device.

Service providers

  • Render hosts our server. Like most hosting providers, it may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and request time) for a short period for security and reliability.
  • Upstash stores the anonymous usage counts described above.
  • Apple distributes the app. If you test through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and feedback you choose to send, under Apple's privacy policy.

We don't sell or share your data with anyone else, and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics.

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have rights to access, correct or delete personal data about you. Because we don't store data that identifies you, there is usually nothing held about you. If you have questions or concerns, email info@agriesg.co.uk. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
